Biography 

Dr. Chengxin Zhang received his PhD in bioinformatics from the Yang Zhang lab at the University of Michigan in 2020. From 2021 to 2023, he was an HHMI postdoctoral associate at the Anna Pyle lab at Yale University. In 2023, he became a research assistant professor at the Lydia Freddolino lab at the University of Michigan. Since 2025, he is a PI at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of China. His research focus on the development of novel algorithms for structure prediction and function annotation of proteins and RNAs.
